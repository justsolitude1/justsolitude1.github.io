01 · Inbound content

Your buyer scrolls past ten posts that sound the same. Then she reads yours.

Think about a founder you'd love to work with. She opens LinkedIn between two meetings and thumbs through her feed. Ten posts in a row have the same hook, the same one-line paragraphs and the same lesson at the end. She can tell a machine wrote them, so she doesn't finish a single one.

Then she lands on a post about the exact argument she had with her co-founder on Tuesday. She reads it to the end. The week after, she reads another. A month later she's in your inbox asking how you work with clients.

That post is the work I do. I find out who your buyers are and what they're dealing with this week, then I write about it in your voice, with your stories. Every post is built to get the right person to start a conversation with you.

Strategy

Start with the buyer

Who they are, what they argue about, and what they'd read to the end. That decides what you post.

Writing

Your voice, your stories

If a post could have come from anyone else's account, it gets rewritten until it couldn't.

Engineering

One post, one job

The first line, the story and the last line are all built to get one reader talking to you.

Who I work with: YC founders, helping them scale their LinkedIn and attract clients through organic content.

02 · Outbound email

She has 47 unread cold emails. Mine is the one she answers.

Now open that same founder's inbox. There are 47 cold emails she hasn't read. Most were written by AI in a few seconds, and they all start the same way: "Hi, I came across your profile." She selects all of them and hits archive.

Mine stays, because it opens with a story about her. Before I write a word, I spend ten minutes reading her posts, her comments and her About section. Then I write about 170 words that would make no sense to anyone else. She reads it, thinks "this person actually read my stuff", and writes back.

This is my outbound system. It runs alongside your content, so the buyers who haven't seen your posts yet still hear from you, and they hear from a person.

What fills her inbox

"I saw your post about buying back time. Great insight!"

What I send

"A buddy of mine has been saying he'll 'start posting once things calm down' for three years."
